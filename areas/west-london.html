TMV servicing in West London

Our office is on Power Road in Chiswick, so West London is where we're quickest. From Hammersmith mansion flats to Hillingdon care homes, this is home turf.

Call 020 3633 6064

West London covers a lot of ground: the dense inner streets of Hammersmith and Fulham, Victorian Ealing, the suburbs of Harrow, the riverside in Richmond and the big open spread of Hillingdon out by Heathrow. We cover all seven boroughs, and because we're based in W4, we're rarely more than a short drive away.

The buildings we see in West London

Converted Victorian and Edwardian houses

Shepherd's Bush, Acton, Ealing and Fulham are full of big terraced houses split into flats or let as HMOs. The plumbing has usually been altered by several different people over the years. TMVs turn up under baths, behind boxed-in pipework and occasionally in places that make us wonder what the last installer was thinking. They're also a classic place for valves that have never been serviced at all.

Mansion blocks and riverside flats

Around Barons Court, West Kensington, Fulham and along the river at Hammersmith and Richmond there are plenty of mansion blocks and purpose-built flats. Older blocks may have central hot water from a communal plant room, which means supply temperatures are worth checking alongside the valves themselves.

Suburban care homes and supported housing

Ealing, Hillingdon, Harrow and Hounslow have a good number of care homes and supported living schemes, many in extended detached houses or purpose-built homes. These usually need TMV3 valves on every resident outlet, and a register that a registered manager can show an inspector without hunting for it.

Schools, airports and everything in between

The western boroughs have large school estates and, out towards Heathrow, hotels, offices and logistics sites with staff showers and washrooms. Hotels near the airport run all year, so we plan room-by-room servicing with housekeeping.

West London water

Thames Water supplies most of West London. Parts of Harrow and Hillingdon, on the north-western edge, are supplied by Affinity Water instead, so it's worth checking your bill if you're not sure. Either way the water out here is hard, and a TMV that's gone a few years without a strainer clean will show it. For your own address, use Thames Water's postcode checker or your supplier's equivalent. Our post on hard water and TMV limescale explains what that scale actually does inside a valve.

What we typically find

  • Scale, lots of it. Strainers blocked to the point of restricting flow are a common sight, especially in shared bathrooms that get hammered every morning.
  • Valves without access. Tiled-in bath valves in refurbished conversions. We'll suggest a neat access panel so the next service isn't a demolition job.
  • Mixed valve types. A portfolio of HMOs will often have a different make in every property. We'll list them all in one asset register.

Getting around

Most inner West London streets are in controlled parking zones, and Hammersmith and Fulham is known for keeping a close eye on them. We check the restrictions before every visit. Outer boroughs like Hillingdon and Harrow are easier for parking but bigger to cross, so we group visits by area to keep travel down. The A4, A40 and the North Circular are part of our daily lives, for better or worse. There's more on how we plan this in planning TMV servicing around CPZs and access.

West London questions

Do you cover TMV servicing in Ealing, Hounslow and the rest of West London?

Yes, we cover all seven West London boroughs: Ealing, Hounslow, Hammersmith and Fulham, Brent, Harrow, Hillingdon and Richmond upon Thames. Our office is in Chiswick, W4, so this is the patch we're closest to. Uxbridge and the Heathrow end take a bit longer to reach, so we group visits there by area.

Who supplies water in West London, and is it hard?

Thames Water supplies most of West London, with Affinity Water supplying parts of Harrow and Hillingdon. The water is hard across the patch, which is why descaling and strainer cleaning are part of every service we do. Your supplier's postcode checker will tell you the hardness at your address.

Can you service TMVs across a portfolio of HMOs in West London?

Yes. We plan the visits by area so one engineer can work through several houses in a day, and you get one register covering every valve, whatever make it is. Licence conditions differ from borough to borough, so check yours with the council. Our post on TMVs in London HMOs covers what landlords tend to ask.

Can you work around hotels and staff shifts near Heathrow?

Yes. Airport hotels and logistics sites run around the clock, so we agree a room or washroom order with housekeeping or the facilities team and work through it in batches. Rooms go back into use the same day, and anything we couldn't get into is listed on the report with a return date.
