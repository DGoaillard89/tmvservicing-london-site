Working in London

We know what a London job actually involves.

The valve's the easy bit. Getting to it, parking near it and doing it without upsetting 80 residents is where local experience pays off.

Hard London water

London's water is famously hard, and limescale is a TMV's worst enemy. It clogs strainers, slows the thermostat and sticks check valves. We descale every time and tell you honestly if your interval needs to be shorter.

Parking and permits

Controlled parking zones, red routes, loading bays shared with three restaurants. We plan parking before we turn up, so you're not paying for an engineer to circle the block.

Access in occupied buildings

Flats, wards, guest rooms, classrooms. We work from your access plan, give residents notice letters if you want them, and come back for the ones we couldn't get into.

Out-of-hours when it suits

Early starts in offices, evenings in clinics, school holidays for schools. We can book work around when your building is quietest.

How it works

Four steps, no surprises.

  1. Tell us what you've got

    Building type, rough valve count, postcode. If you don't know how many TMVs you have, that's fine. Lots of people don't.

  2. Fixed quote and a plan

    We price it, then agree access, parking and timing. Notice letters for residents too, if you want them.

  3. We service every valve

    Tested, cleaned, descaled, set and fail-safe checked. Anything we can't fix there and then gets flagged with a price.

  4. Records you can use

    Certificates, logbook entries and an updated asset register, plus the date your next service is due.
