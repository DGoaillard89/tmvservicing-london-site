East London has seen a huge amount of new building over the last couple of decades. Canary Wharf, Stratford and the Royal Docks have filled with towers, student blocks and serviced apartments, while Hackney and Bethnal Green keep their terraces and estates. Further out, Redbridge, Barking & Dagenham and Havering are more suburban. All of it has TMVs, and in newer blocks they're easy to forget about once the developer hands over.

The buildings we see in East London

New-build towers and build-to-rent

Around Canary Wharf, Stratford, the Isle of Dogs and the Royal Docks, high-rise blocks often have TMVs on every bath and sometimes on basins and showers too, hidden behind magnetic or screw-fix panels. Many were fitted at construction and quietly forgotten. Build-to-rent operators and managing agents increasingly want a proper register and service programme, and that's where we come in.

Purpose-built student accommodation

Student blocks around Mile End, Stratford and Whitechapel have hundreds of near-identical en-suites. Summer, between academic years, is the natural window for servicing when rooms are empty, though summer lets and conference guests can complicate that. We plan by floor so cleaners and maintenance teams can follow behind.

Council estates and housing associations

Tower Hamlets, Hackney, Newham and Barking & Dagenham have large social housing portfolios, from post-war estates to new mixed-tenure schemes. Sheltered and supported housing blocks often have a TMV on every outlet. With social landlords under more scrutiny on home safety, a clear service record matters.

Serviced apartments, hotels and offices

Docklands hotels and aparthotels and the Canary Wharf offices all run busy showers and washrooms. Early starts and weekend work are common requests here.

Suburban homes, schools and care

In Redbridge and Havering we see more care homes, schools and family houses converted to HMOs. Much easier parking, much longer drives between jobs.

East London water

Thames Water supplies most of inner East London, drawing on the Thames and the River Lee. Further out, Essex & Suffolk Water supplies parts of Havering, Barking & Dagenham and Redbridge, so the name on your bill depends on where you are. Both are hard-water areas. Check your exact address with Thames Water's postcode checker or on your supplier's website.

What we typically find

  • Valves no one knew about. New-build flats where residents (and sometimes managing agents) don't know the TMV exists until we find it behind the bath panel.
  • Boosted supplies in towers. High-rise water systems are pumped, so supply pressures vary floor by floor. We check that the valve suits the pressures it's actually getting.
  • Scale on valves untouched since handover. A new-build TMV that's had five years of hard water and no service will usually need a proper strip-down, not just a temperature check.

Getting around

Docklands developments often have private estate roads, concierge desks and strict loading bay booking, so we book in advance. Hackney and Tower Hamlets have extensive CPZs. Further east, the A13 and A12 are the main routes. We group jobs so one engineer can cover a cluster of blocks in a day rather than criss-crossing the patch.

TMV servicing borough by borough

From Shoreditch to the Essex border, the six East London boroughs couldn't be much more different.

Victorian terraces in Stoke Newington, Dalston and Clapton, warehouse conversions in Shoreditch and Hackney Wick, and large council estates throughout. Controlled parking covers most of the borough, and plenty of streets are filtered to through traffic, so we plan the route as well as the bay.

Tower Hamlets

{{pc:Tower Hamlets}}

Canary Wharf's offices and the towers of the Isle of Dogs sit alongside dense estates in Bethnal Green, Poplar and Stepney, the Royal London Hospital in Whitechapel and Queen Mary's campus at Mile End. Docklands blocks usually mean concierge sign-in and booked loading bays.

Newham

{{pc:Newham}}

Stratford and the Olympic Park have filled with build-to-rent, student halls and hotels, and the Royal Docks have more around ExCeL. The older terraces of Forest Gate, East Ham and Plaistow have a lot of private rented homes, and Newham has long licensed its private landlords, so clear records matter.

Redbridge

{{pc:Redbridge}}

Ilford town centre has new flats and offices, while Woodford, Wanstead and Gants Hill are largely suburban houses, schools and care homes. Parking is easier than inner East London, though Ilford's town centre is busy.

Barking and Dagenham

{{pc:Barking and Dagenham}}

Home to the Becontree estate, a vast inter-war council estate, plus newer schemes such as Barking Riverside. Council and housing association homes, sheltered housing and schools make up much of the TMV work. The A13 is the main route in.

Havering

{{pc:Havering}}

London's most easterly borough, with Romford as its big town centre, plenty of suburban housing and green belt beyond. Care homes and schools are common, journeys from W4 are long, so we group Havering visits together.

Greenwich, Lewisham and Bexley are south of the river, so they sit in our South London patch. Waltham Forest is in North London and the City is in Central London.

East London questions

Do you service TMVs in new-build flats in Canary Wharf and Stratford?

Yes. Many East London towers have a TMV on every bath, and sometimes on basins and showers too, fitted at construction and often never serviced since. We find them, test them, check they suit the boosted supply pressures in the block, and give the managing agent a register. Our post on TMVs in high-rise blocks goes into more detail.

Who supplies water in East London?

Thames Water supplies most of East London, and Essex & Suffolk Water supplies parts of Havering, Barking & Dagenham and Redbridge. Both supply hard water, so TMVs here need regular strainer cleaning and descaling. Your bill shows your supplier, and their website has a postcode checker for hardness.

When is the best time to service TMVs in student accommodation?

Usually the summer, between academic years, when most rooms are empty. Summer lets and conference bookings can eat into that window, so it's worth agreeing dates early. We work floor by floor so cleaning and maintenance teams can follow straight behind us.

Can you plan TMV servicing for a housing association across East London?

Yes. We plan block by block, send resident letters, record every valve in a register and keep chasing no-access homes until they're done or you tell us to stop. Social landlords are under closer scrutiny on home safety, so a clear service record helps. Our post on Awaab's Law and hot water safety covers the background.
