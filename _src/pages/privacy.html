What we collect

When you use our enquiry form, we collect your name, email address, phone number, postcode and whatever you tell us about your enquiry. We also record which of our websites the enquiry came from.

Why we use it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quote and, if you go ahead, arrange and carry out the work. Our lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries. We don't sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Where it's stored

Enquiries are passed securely through Cloudflare and stored in our customer records system, Airtable. These providers process data on our behalf under their own data protection terms.

How long we keep it

If you don't become a customer, we delete enquiry details within 24 months. Customer records are kept as long as we need them for our legal, insurance and compliance obligations.

Cookies and similar technologies

Analytics and advertising cookies stay switched off unless you agree to them in the banner you see on your first visit. There are two kinds you can say yes or no to:

  • Analytics (Google Analytics): tells us how many people visit and which pages they find useful.
  • Advertising (Google Ads): lets us see whether our ads lead to enquiries, and show our ads again to people who've already been on the site.

They're delivered through Google Tag Manager, which itself only loads once you've accepted. We remember your answer in your browser's local storage, and you can change it whenever you like using "Cookie settings" in the footer. For how Google uses this information, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

When you arrive from an advert or a tagged link, the address may carry campaign tags (for example a Google click ID or "utm" labels). If you then send us an enquiry, we include those tags with it so we can tell which ad or link worked. If you've accepted cookies, they're also held in your browser until you close the tab.

Fonts come from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers to fetch them.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Call us on 020 3633 6064 or write to us at the address above. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
